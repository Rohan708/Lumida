The problem

Databases break in silence.

Uptime monitors check that the database answers. Nobody checks whether what's inside still makes sense.

Hidden links

Tables point at each other through columns like cust_no or ITEM_CD, but no foreign key says so. So nothing checks it.

Undocumented structure01

Silent breakage

A nightly load runs halfway, a form stops saving postal codes, a migration drops a key. The dashboards keep showing numbers, just wrong ones.

Wrong, not down02

Nobody knows normal

No documentation, no tests, and the person who built it left. You can't alert on "unusual" if nobody knows what usual is.

How it works

Every night, four steps.

No tests to write first. Lumida works out the rules of your data from the data itself, and asks you only where it can't.

  1. 01 // Discover

    Map it

    Reads the structure and finds the relationships nobody declared, from names, matching values and your app's own queries.

    Undeclared links, with confidence

  2. 02 // Learn

    Learn normal

    Learns each column's usual empties, ranges and values, and each table's weekday rhythm, from its own history.

    Measured inside your database

  3. 03 // Ask

    Ask you

    Asks a few questions where your answer changes what it checks, with its best guess filled in. Each answer becomes a check.

    Plus your dbt docs, if you have them

  4. 04 // Watch

    Tell you

    Checks every night, sends one digest with only what's new, and explains what each problem means and what it touches.

    Slack, or the web app

AI discovers. People approve. Lumida enforces.

What it finds

Eight kinds of problems, every night.

Each with its own evidence, its own severity, and a health score per table, schema and database.

01

Structure

Missing keys, dropped columns, changed types, unindexed links.

02

Relationships

Orphan rows and duplicates, even where no foreign key was declared.

03

Values

Sudden empties, impossible numbers, “USA” creeping in next to “US”.

04

Business rules

“Shipped after ordered”, “total = sum of lines”. AI proposes, you approve.

05

Freshness & volume

Loads that didn't run or ran halfway, aware of weekdays and closed days.

06

Unusual rows

An order line with quantity 500 where 1–3 is normal, with its row IDs.

07

Copies that disagree

The warehouse copy is missing orders the app database has.

08

Personal data

A map of where it lives, and a flag where it shouldn't be.

Security & privacy

Your data stays inside your network.

Lumida runs on your machines, with its own small Postgres for what it learns. No account, no telemetry. And it's open source: your security team can read every line.

Read-only, with timeouts

Lumida logs in with a read-only user (setup scripts for every database), opens read-only transactions, and stops any query that runs too long. It never writes to your database.

Counted inside your database

The counting happens where the data is. What comes back is counts, statistics and the IDs of the rows involved, not the rows themselves.

No row values sent anywhere

Values aren't logged. Keeping example values (like the most common categories) can be switched off. The optional AI features see structure and statistics, not your rows.

People approve, people log in

AI only proposes; a person approves every rule. Personal logins with roles, and an audit log of who confirmed, rejected or changed what.

Install

Running in minutes.

On a server, one docker compose up. On a laptop, pip install lumida and lumida serve: the browser does the rest. Add a database in the browser, test the connection, press Run now. From then on it runs by itself every night, at the time you choose.

No account, no sign-up, no telemetry. It runs on your machines and talks only to your databases.
